The BaiYun Method™

Six steps, repeated at every level, from your first word to real fluency.

01

Learn

Build core vocabulary and grammar with structured, bite-sized lessons.

02

Speak

Practice in guided conversations from your very first class.

03

Listen

Train your ear on native speakers, not just textbook audio.

04

Connect

Understand the culture that gives the language its meaning.

05

Apply

Use what you've learned in real-life scenarios — travel, work, exams.

06

Advance

Move up through levels with regular assessment and feedback.
